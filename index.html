        How it works

        1. Drop your PDF

          Pick the loan package from your computer. The sorting happens right here in your browser.

        2. It measures every page

          Letter pages are 11 inches tall. Legal pages are 14. Each page goes in the pile that matches its size.

        3. Print and restack

          Print one pile, swap the paper, print the other. The restack guide shows where each Legal page goes back in.

        Is my loan package safe?

        Yes. The page has a built-in rule that blocks it from sending anything over the internet. Your PDF is read on your computer and the two new PDFs are made on your computer. To test it, turn off your Wi-Fi and sort a file.

        What if a page is not Letter or Legal?

        It goes in the closest pile and gets a dashed outline on the page map, so you can check it before you print.

        What about locked PDFs?

        Many packages are locked but open without a password. Those sort right away. If yours needs a password, the page asks for it.

        Do I still need to check my pages?

        Yes. Always count your printed sheets and compare them with the original package before a signing. This tool helps with printing. It does not check documents.
